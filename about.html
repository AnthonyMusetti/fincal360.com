📊 What is FinLit360?

FinLit360 is a comprehensive suite of financial calculators and planning tools, all in one place, connected to a single net worth picture. No ads, no data collection, no sign-up. Your numbers never leave your device.

🔒 Your Data is Private

All data is stored in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server. Nothing is shared. Clearing your browser cache is the only way data gets removed. Use the Export feature on the home page to back up your data.

🧮 What's Included

Retirement planning · Mortgage calculator · Auto loan · Budget planner · Debt & DTI analysis with payoff strategies · Savings tracker · Loan calculator · Rental property analysis with IRR, MIRR, MOIC, DSCR, and tax modeling.

📤 Export Everything

Export any calculator to PDF for sharing or printing. Export all your data to a single Excel spreadsheet for backup from the home page, and restore it anytime. The budget and debt tabs also accept direct XLS uploads.
